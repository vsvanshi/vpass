VPass Support

VPass is a credential and authenticator (TOTP) manager for Mac and iPhone. Your data is stored in the Apple Keychain and, with iCloud Keychain enabled, syncs securely across your own Apple devices.

Common questions

My credentials don’t appear on my other device. Make sure both devices are signed into the same Apple ID and have iCloud Keychain enabled (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Passwords and Keychain). Sync can take a few moments.

How do I add an authenticator (TOTP)? Open a credential, tap Edit, and use “Scan QR Code” in the Authenticator section, or enter the secret manually.

How is my data protected? Everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud Keychain. VPass has no servers and collects no data. See our Privacy Policy.
